AI services advertised on underground forums often make the same promise: they provide answers to requests that mainstream AI assistants would refuse.

Unit 42 examined WormGPT 4 and KawaiiGPT, documenting ransomware code generated by WormGPT 4 and phishing messages, lateral-movement scripts, and data-exfiltration scripts generated by KawaiiGPT.

Trellix explored the business behind underground AI, reporting on APEX AI, MessiahGPT, and DarkGPT, alongside a crypter advertised as AI-enhanced and an underground market for session cookies and access to commercial AI services.

Omega AI takes this sales pitch further by offering a workspace that combines chat and code generation, a prompt market, vulnerability scanning, code execution, media creation, and guidance on how to sell the resulting content or tools. What makes it interesting is how these features are combined into a single interface, which could reduce the effort required to set up and switch between different tools.
